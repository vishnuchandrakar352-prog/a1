about us

A quiet guide to a slow craft

Bespoke is an independent website about tailoring. We explain how clothes are cut, fitted and finished, so you can talk to a tailor with confidence.

i.Independent

We sell nothing and use no affiliate links. No tailor or mill pays to be mentioned.

ii.Private

The size finder and cloth picker run in your browser and store nothing about you.

iii.Careful

Every guide is checked by a second editor and dated when it changes.
